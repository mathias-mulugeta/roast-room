# Pitch scripts

Source: Hormozi seat consult, 2026-10-13. The seat is a simulation built from Alex Hormozi's public work. It is not the real person. Its one quoted line is verbatim from his brain (S069).

Status: **Recommended**, not yet tested with a real owner. Context: [decisions.md](../decisions.md) (R1 to R10).

## The call

Stop writing a pitch. Write a question list and a 3-minute close. You have no proof yet, so the sale is "I did the work first, now look at it."

## The offer

- $199 a month, paid up front.
- 30-day refund on the first month. Total refund exposure under $1,000.
- Five spots only.
- Never cut the rate. Change terms instead.

## DM script

> "[Name], I made 15 posts for [Business] using your real menu, photos and reviews. Free, no catch. Want me to send them? If you like them, I'll run all your posting for $199 a month, paid up front, and refund you within 30 days if you don't want it. I'm taking 5 businesses this month."

Send the posts. Then ask: "Which of these would you actually post?"

## Walk-in or phone script (under 3 minutes)

1. **Open:** "Hi, I'm Matt. I help local [type] get posted without the owner doing it. Got 2 minutes?"
2. **Clarify:** "Who handles your posting now? When did you last post? What happens when you're slammed?"
3. **Label:** "So it's you, late at night, and it slips. Right?"
4. **Show the posts:** "These are yours. Which one would you post?"
5. **Offer:** "I run all of it. You approve or ignore. $199 a month, paid today. If you don't want it in 30 days, I refund the first month. Five spots."
6. **Close:** "Card or invoice?" Then stay silent.

## "Too expensive"

Don't defend the price. Ask: "Compared to what? What does a missed week cost you?"

Then find out whether it is money or doubt that it works. If it is doubt, change a term, not the rate:

- "Pay for 3 months now, and I'll add 4 extra posts a week."
- "Month one is refundable, month two onward is firm."

If they still say no, ask for a referral and move on.

## Numbers to hold

- 3 owners for the post test. 5 sales needed.
- At a 20% close rate you need about 25 conversations for 5 sales.
- Over an 80% close rate means $199 is too low.
- Month 1 churn runs 20% or more, so onboarding is the product.
- 5 x $199 = $995 in the first 30 days against roughly $0 of cost.

## This week

- Build the 15-post test for 3 owners.
- Do 100 outreach actions a day, warm contacts first.
- Log every "no" as money, partner, or self-doubt.

## Where the lens misfires

Software at $199 is a lower-ticket game. Treat this as a service first and automate later.

## His question back

Which three owners can you name today, and what's the last thing each one posted?
