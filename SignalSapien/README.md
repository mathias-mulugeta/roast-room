# SignalSapien

An AI-powered social media management tool for small local businesses. This folder tracks the questions asked, the answers received, and the decisions made.

## Scope

This folder is only for questions and decisions about the AI SMMA (the AI-powered social media management business, including its SaaS pricing and pitch). Questions about anything else do not go here.

## Files

- [pitch/pitch-scripts.md](pitch/pitch-scripts.md): the DM and walk-in scripts and how to handle "too expensive".
- [questions.md](questions.md): every question, who or what answered it, and the answer in short.
- [decisions.md](decisions.md): each decision, its status, why, and what would change it.

## How to keep it

- Add a question when you ask one. Add a decision only when you confirm it.
- Council advice starts as **Recommended**. It becomes **Decided** when you say so.
- Council seats are simulations built from public work. They are not the real people.
- Full council sessions live in `sessions/`. Link to them instead of copying them.
